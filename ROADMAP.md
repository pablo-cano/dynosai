# Roadmap

The 0.13.0 public beta goal is stability and real-world feedback, not immediate expansion of autonomous authority.

## 0.13.x public beta line

Priorities:

- compatibility fixes discovered by real users;
- documentation and onboarding;
- additional language/stack validation profiles and examples;
- packaging/CI portability;
- focused refactors with characterization tests;
- broader greenfield/brownfield repositories.

## Candidate post-stable research

- independent second-model semantic code review;
- stronger static-analysis/security/coverage integrations;
- configurable build/lint/type-check discovery by stack;
- more rigorous structural token estimator calibration;
- Predictive Router counterfactual validation and safe authority criteria;
- decomposition of the largest orchestration modules without public-contract changes.

Predictive routing should remain shadow-only until evidence demonstrates that autonomous decisions improve cost/quality without unsafe downshifts or missed capability escalations.
